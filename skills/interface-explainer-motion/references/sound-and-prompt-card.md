# Jeff-style sound cues and prompt card

## Sound design for visual actions

The user wants audible, subtle UI cues in Jeff-style software tutorials: a click on each visible mouse click, a short movement sound for each push-in or zoom, and typing sounds during visible typing. Make a cue sheet from the actual timeline, then place effects on separate editable audio tracks. Synchronize a click's transient with the moment the pointer presses; let a short whoosh start with the camera move and end as it settles. Synchronize keystrokes with visible text entry, stop during pauses and avoid a distracting continuous loop. Chinese text entry and pasted text may not map one sound per displayed character; use a natural cadence that follows what is visibly happening.

For typing, the user rejected the former synthetic tick: use a real keyboard recording with natural variation in key tone and rhythm. In ChatCut, search the sound-effects library for keyboard typing; candidates include `library:sound:keyboard-typing-loop` and `library:sound:slow-dell-keyboard-typing`. Audition and trim a suitable portion, start and stop with the visible text, and avoid presenting a rapid loop during a visible pause. If unavailable, obtain a suitable licensed recording or the user's own recording; do not fall back silently to the rejected synthetic tick. Stock assets are accessed through the host library, not bundled or redistributed in this skill. Similarity to Jeff's exact recording must be judged by listening and not assumed.

Starter files [mouse-click](../assets/sfx/mouse-click.wav) and [zoom-whoosh](../assets/sfx/zoom-whoosh.wav) are original synthetic sounds, not extracted Jeff audio and not verified matches to the creator's sound library. Replace or adjust their tone, length and volume when the reference, user or material warrants it. `python3 scripts/make_sfx.py` regenerates them. Balance below narration, apply short fades when needed, and keep headroom to prevent clipping. Do not mute the narration or add effects solely to create activity. The effects must be present in the final mix, not only described in a storyboard; review with audio and confirm synchronization.
