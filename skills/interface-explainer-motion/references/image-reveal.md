## Adaptation for WorkBuddy

The user wants an engaging transition when WorkBuddy produces an image, potentially by clicking it open. Show the image in context; use a pointer press only when the demonstration actually shows one; dim surrounding UI and grow the same image into a centered large view; hold the whole result, then return or continue. Match sounds to [action roles](sound-and-prompt-card.md#sound-design-for-visual-actions): click on a shown press, short whoosh/swish on movement, light pop/chime on the clear reveal. These cues are the user's adaptation, not claims about the reference clip. Align to narration without hiding captions or cropping the result.

This user's videos are short-form and need brisk pacing. For this image reveal, the user asks for the entire move to take about two seconds, with a smaller, slightly blurred initial thumbnail that creates anticipation. Gradually remove the blur as it opens and reveal the actual result sharply in the enlarged view; it stays clear if it returns to thumbnail size after being revealed. The blur should not obscure the final payoff. Keep the [slowly drifting sparse-star background](visual-grammar.md#sparse-star-background-for-this-users-series) during enlargement. Apply the series' [fast push/pull timing](jeff-person-openings.md#推拉速度与开头三秒) to the moving portions only: a workable updated beat is click around 0.4s, zoom around 0.52–0.67s, a readable full-image hold, then return around 1.65–1.80s. These already-halved movement durations are the new baseline; do not halve them again on every reuse. Adapt to speech and the content while keeping the whole reveal around two seconds; spend the saved motion time on the clear result. Dense details may need a different presentation rather than silently extending this transition.

For this user's finished videos, the [post-opening camera limit](jeff-person-openings.md#10秒后的运镜频次) takes precedence over the paired zoom-in/zoom-back example above. At or after 10 seconds, an enlargement consumes one camera move and a return would consume another: use at most the permitted enlargement, then hold it or cut to genuinely new content without a second scale move inside eight seconds. If no camera move is available, show the legible result directly. Adapt a reusable reveal template's return phase rather than importing its full push/pull sequence unchanged.

If the workbuddy-simulator skill is available, `assets/workbuddy-image-reveal.html` is an editable standalone starting point with `imageSrc` and time parameters in `image-reveal-config`. Its embedded coffee poster and UI are fictional; replace the poster with the user's actual image where available. If the user requests no on-screen simulation badge, remove the template's baked-in badge from the copy used for the render and explain the simulated nature in the delivery. It produces silent video through the simulator renderer; add and mix effects in the editing timeline. Keep the UI, image, caption and sounds separately editable where practical. Preserve image proportions and test portrait, landscape or square assets as relevant. A keyframed real recording may be preferable where accuracy requires it.

Inspect the starting image, click frame, midpoint, settled enlargement and exit, as well as the full render with sound. Verify the enlarged frame does not crop the user image or obscure captions, and that timing and audio match what is visible.
